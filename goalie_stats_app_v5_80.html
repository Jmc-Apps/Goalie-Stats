Benchmark Calculation Rules

    
    
      
  • Save Rate and Defence Rate use each goalkeeper's average across qualifying matches, so one goalkeeper cannot dominate the benchmark by contributing more matches.
    • 
      
  • Benchmark heat maps include every anonymised shot from qualifying matches.
    • 
      
  • Small samples remain visible and are marked Limited Data.
    • 
      
  • Benchmark figures unlock only after opting in and completing a successful eligible upload.
    • 
    

    
Limited Data: fewer than 5 goalkeepers, 15 matches or 100 shots
